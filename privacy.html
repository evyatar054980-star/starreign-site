Star Reign — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-13 · Developer: Evyatar Barnoy, Israel ("we") · Contact: evyatar054980@gmail.com

In short

Star Reign has no accounts, no sign-in, no purchases and no ads in its current version. It never asks for your name, email, contacts or location. Your progress is saved on your own device. To improve the game's difficulty and flow we record anonymous gameplay statistics through ByteBrew, a game-analytics service; they are tied to a random player id, not to you, and are deleted after at most 24 months. We do not sell data.

1. What is stored on your device

Your game progress (the planet you are on, your upgrades, coins, best try, settings, the time you have played on the current planet for the statistics in section 2, and whether you have finished or skipped the Training Camp) and your daily gifts (how many you have claimed and the day of the last claim, your revives, the ×2 coins boost's timers, the gifts you hold and when the timed ones run out, and the coins and length of your last five tries, which set the size of a coin chest) are kept in the app's local storage on Android, or in the browser's local storage on the web. The daily gifts turn over at midnight by your device's clock and time zone. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site data deletes all of it. We cannot read it from our side. The web version sets only ByteBrew's analytics cookies described in section 2 (two that hold the random player id, and one that records your No if you switch the statistics off) and no advertising cookies; cookies set by the website that hosts the game are that website's own (section 4).

2. Gameplay statistics (ByteBrew)

What we send. While you play, the game sends these events to ByteBrew: a try started and a try ended (with the planet, which try it is, the percentage of the road you reached, your coins and your ships, and whether you cleared the planet), an upgrade bought (which one, its new level and its cost), a boss beaten (which boss), a planet conquered (how many tries and minutes it took), each step of the Training Camp you finish (which step and the seconds since the training began), the Training Camp skipped (the step you were on and the seconds since the training began), a ×2 coins boost started (from its button, on your own or after the game pointed to it, or from a daily gift), a daily gift claimed (its day and the gift), a revive offered (whether you took it and how far the try had come), and the session number. In the EU/EEA and Switzerland, and whenever the game cannot tell which country you are in, it asks you before any statistics are sent, with a small Yes/No bar on the start screen; nothing goes to ByteBrew unless you choose Yes, and you can change your answer in the game's settings. Each event carries a random player id that the ByteBrew software creates on first launch and keeps on your device: on the web in ByteBrew's cookies in your browser (named bb_u_id and bb_u_h_init), on Android in the app's own storage. It is not linked to your name, email or any account, and we never ask for those. To remove the web id, clear the site's cookies or site data in your browser; the next launch creates a new one that has nothing to do with the old.

What arrives with it. On the web, each event goes with a session id, the game's version, the ByteBrew software's version, your screen size, your browser's language setting and a country code that the software derives from that language setting. Like any request on the internet, the events reach ByteBrew's servers with your network (IP) address, from which ByteBrew may derive a country. In the Android app, ByteBrew's software also records the device model and type, the operating-system version and the connection type; the Android build does not request the advertising-ID permission, so the software cannot read the advertising ID. If a later version changes any of this, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will say so first.

Why. We use these statistics to see where players stop, which upgrades help and how long a planet takes, so we can tune the difficulty and the flow of the game. We do not use them for advertising, and we do not sell them.

Who processes it. ByteBrew (ByteBrew Inc., United States) processes the data on our behalf under its privacy policy and a data-processing agreement; we are the data controller. ByteBrew does not sell the data. Its servers are in the United States; transfers from the EU, the UK and Switzerland rest on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in that agreement.

How long. ByteBrew keeps the gameplay data for at most 24 months from collection and then deletes it; we keep only aggregated figures (for example "the average player clears Mars in 21 tries") with no player id in them.

Your choice. Switch STATS off in the game's settings (also the pause menu) at any time; from then on nothing is sent, and on the web a cookie named bb_tr_on remembers that No. To have what was already stored deleted, write to us (section 9) and we have ByteBrew delete what is kept under your player id. Deleting the app, or on the web clearing the site's cookies or site data, creates a new random id on the next launch; the old one is no longer linked to anything on your device.

3. Advertising

The current version shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID. If a future version shows ads (for example a rewarded ad for extra coins), this policy will be updated first to name the ad provider and what it collects.

4. The web version on CrazyGames

On crazygames.com the game is hosted by CrazyGames. Their website's cookies, accounts, ads and any cloud save tied to a CrazyGames account are governed by CrazyGames' privacy policy and managed through its own cookie banner. The game itself sends only what sections 1–2 describe, and only while it runs on CrazyGames' pages; its only cookies are ByteBrew's analytics cookies (section 2), and it sets no advertising cookies. CrazyGames, as the host, can see the same gameplay statistics in our ByteBrew dashboard; that access covers the web version only and nothing that identifies you.

5. Children

Star Reign is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Where the law gives you rights over your data (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Israel's Privacy Protection Law), you can ask us what we hold, ask for it to be deleted, or object to its use, by writing to evyatar054980@gmail.com. Because the statistics are tied only to a random player id, tell us the device, the app version and the dates you played (and the player id, if the game's settings screen shows one) so we can find it; we then have ByteBrew delete it. Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your device.

7. Security and transfer

Any data sent from the game travels encrypted (HTTPS). ByteBrew stores it encrypted in the United States; the safeguards in section 2 apply to the transfer.
