Star Reign — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-04 · Developer: Evyatar Barnoy, Israel ("we") · Contact: evyatar054980@gmail.com

In short

Star Reign has no accounts, no sign-in, no purchases and no ads in its current version. It never asks for your name, email, contacts or location. Your progress is saved on your own device. To improve the game's difficulty and flow we record anonymous gameplay statistics through ByteBrew, a game-analytics service; they are tied to a random player id, not to you, and are deleted after at most 24 months. We do not sell data.

1. What is stored on your device

Your game progress (the planet you are on, your upgrades, coins, best try and settings) is kept in the app's local storage on Android, or in the browser's local storage on the web. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site data deletes it. We cannot read it from our side.

2. Gameplay statistics (ByteBrew)

What we send. While you play, the game sends these events to ByteBrew: a try started and a try ended (with the planet, the percentage of the road you reached, your coins and your ships), an upgrade bought, a boss beaten, a planet conquered, and the session number. Each event carries a random player id that the ByteBrew software creates on first launch and keeps on your device; it is not linked to your name, email or any account, and we never ask for those.

What arrives with it. Like any request on the internet, the events reach ByteBrew's servers with your network (IP) address; ByteBrew derives a country from it. ByteBrew's software also records technical details of the device: the device model and type, the operating-system version, the language, the screen resolution and the connection type. The Android build does not request the advertising-ID permission, so the ByteBrew software cannot read the advertising ID; if a later version does, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will say so first.

Why. We use these statistics to see where players stop, which upgrades help and how long a planet takes, so we can tune the difficulty and the flow of the game. We do not use them for advertising, and we do not sell them.

Who processes it. ByteBrew (ByteBrew Inc., United States) processes the data on our behalf under its privacy policy and a data-processing agreement; we are the data controller. ByteBrew does not sell the data. Its servers are in the United States; transfers from the EU, the UK and Switzerland rest on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in that agreement.

How long. ByteBrew keeps the gameplay data for at most 24 months from collection and then deletes it; we keep only aggregated figures (for example "the average player clears Mars in 21 tries") with no player id in them.

Your choice. You can stop the statistics at any time by writing to us (section 9); we then have ByteBrew delete what is stored under your player id. A "Share gameplay stats" switch inside the game's settings is planned, and this policy will say so when it is there. Deleting the app or clearing the site data creates a new random id on the next launch; the old one is no longer linked to anything on your device.

3. Advertising

The current version shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID. If a future version shows ads (for example a rewarded ad for extra coins), this policy will be updated first to name the ad provider and what it collects.

4. The web version on CrazyGames

On crazygames.com the game is hosted by CrazyGames. Their website's cookies, accounts, ads and any cloud save tied to a CrazyGames account are governed by CrazyGames' privacy policy. The game itself sends only what sections 1–2 describe. CrazyGames, as the host, can see the same gameplay statistics in our ByteBrew dashboard; that access covers the web version only and nothing that identifies you.

5. Children

Star Reign is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Where the law gives you rights over your data (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Israel's Privacy Protection Law), you can ask us what we hold, ask for it to be deleted, or object to its use, by writing to evyatar054980@gmail.com. Because the statistics are tied only to a random player id, tell us the device, the app version and the dates you played (and the player id, if the game's settings screen shows one) so we can find it; we then have ByteBrew delete it. Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your device.

7. Security and transfer

Any data sent from the game travels encrypted (HTTPS). ByteBrew stores it encrypted in the United States; the safeguards in section 2 apply to the transfer.

8. Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new effective date.
